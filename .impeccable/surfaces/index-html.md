## Direction contract

THESIS: Due dates turn a flat task collection into a readable priority inbox. A top navigation, grouped task rows and a focus-week rail replace the incumbent sidebar/statistic tiles and avoid NOVA's project-board-first layout.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral white/ink surfaces, restrained blue on day selection and task deadlines, red only for overdue state. Self-hosted Geist, 14px task text, flat 12–16px surfaces, familiar native selects, semantic buttons and an accessible side-sheet form. Light serves long daytime reading; dark remains an equal saved preference.

STORY: Review what needs attention, filter/search, complete a task or open its details. Focus on a date, change view without changing the data, and back up the local workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact top bar holds the brand, four real hash destinations and preferences. A centered 1200px workspace holds a noun heading and New task, a status count strip, search/filters/view controls, then grouped task rows beside a narrow next-seven-days and summary rail. Rows expose title, priority, due date, completion and status. The signature is a clickable week of real due counts that scopes the inbox, with a visible reset. Settings is a separate screen, never fake account links.
